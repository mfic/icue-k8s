# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone. Participants are expected to treat each other with respect, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Accept feedback gracefully and offer it the same way
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users
- Assume good intent

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks or derogatory comments
- Public or private harassment of any kind
- Deliberately disruptive or dishonest behaviour

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported by opening a private issue or contacting the maintainer directly via GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and acted on at the maintainer's discretion. Severe or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban from the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other forum associated with this repository.
